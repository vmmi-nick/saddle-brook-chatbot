Welcome

This is a simple demonstration page showing how the Saddle Brook Constituent Chatbot appears when it is added to a borough webpage. A small card appears in the corner a moment after the page loads, saying what the assistant is and inviting a tap. Tapping the card opens the full chat. If you dismiss the card, or close the chat, it folds down to the Ask Saddle Brook button in the corner, and it stays closed while you move around the site.

Try asking it when the next council meeting is, how garbage pickup works, or use the Report an issue button inside the chat to prepare a report to call in.
